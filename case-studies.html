Highlands Virtual Assist

Three move-ins in a single month from website chat, with zero placement fees.

    Website traffic arrived, but most families left without a name or number. Filling those apartments often meant third-party placement agencies charging roughly $4,000 per move-in.

SS
A Senior Living Operator
Assisted Living · Multi-state
Senior SnapSign

Move-in paperwork cut from four hours to about fifteen minutes.

  1. The challenge

    Every move-in meant 17 separate forms: printed, signed, scanned, and chased. Each one consumed more than four hours of a salesperson's or Executive Director's day.

  2. The approach

    SnapSign digitized the full move-in packet. Every form, agreement, and disclosure is delivered, signed, and stored in one place, with live visibility into who has signed and what is blocking move-in.

  3. The outcome

    Move-in paperwork now takes about fifteen minutes. The three-plus hours returned per move-in go where they belong: helping the resident feel at home in their new community.

A Senior Living Operator
Multiple markets · Georgia, South Carolina & North Carolina
Web & Marketing

Ranking first or second in every market, with 17 calls a month on a $500 ad budget.

  1. The challenge

    Families increasingly ask AI assistants and search engines where a parent should live, and the operator's communities were invisible in those answers. Census growth leaned on paid placement agencies.

  2. The approach

    AEO and SEO work tuned each community's presence for how AI and search actually answer local questions, backed by targeted Google Ads across 140+ keywords and a unique toll-free number so every call could be attributed.

  3. The outcome

    The communities now rank first or second in each of their markets. The tracked number brings in around 17 calls a month, converting one to two residents monthly, with no placement fees involved.
