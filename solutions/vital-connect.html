What it does

Care, coordination, and connection in one place.

Continuous wellness signals

Resident health data captured and surfaced daily, so your team spots trends before they become emergencies.

Family-facing updates

Secure, branded updates sent directly to family members through a mobile app, keeping loved ones informed without flooding your staff with calls.

Care coordination

Shift handoffs, care plans, and team communications stay in sync across every caregiver, every shift.

Smart alerts

Threshold-based notifications route to the right person at the right time, without creating alert fatigue for your entire team.

Physician data sharing

Resident health data can be shared directly with attending physicians and outside healthcare providers, keeping the full care team aligned.

Operational insights

Census trends, care volume, and population-level wellness analytics give your leadership team a clear picture of community health.

Patient portal access

Residents and families get secure portal access to health records, care notes, and wellness history on their own timeline.

HIPAA-aware by design

Clinical evidence

Real results from real communities.

An independent study conducted by a geriatric cardiologist team in Macon, GA documented the impact of Vital Connect across a resident population.

Improved blood pressure across the board

Over half of residents showed measurable improvement in both systolic and diastolic blood pressure over the study period.

Fewer high-risk residents

The share of residents classified as hypertensive dropped meaningfully, reflecting genuine population-level health improvement.

More residents in the healthy range

Normal and high-normal blood pressure classifications increased, showing the platform's effect on proactive, preventive care.
