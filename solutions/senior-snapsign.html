The Problem

Move-in day shouldn't start with a stack of paper.

400+ pages per resident

Residency agreements, financial disclosures, HIPAA forms, care plans, advance directives. Every move-in becomes a binder.

Families sign in person, often twice

Documents get missed, initials forgotten, and families end up driving back to the community to fix what should have been simple.

Documents live in filing cabinets

When you need to find a signed form months later, it's a manual hunt. Audits and family requests grind to a halt.

Inside SnapSign

The platform your team works in.

From first prospect to signed agreement, every step of the move-in lives in one place.

01 · Move-in Pipeline

Every resident's status, at a glance.

The pipeline tracks every prospect from first contact through move-in: New Prospect, Assessed, Ready to Sign, Signed, and Admitted. Your team always knows exactly where each resident stands.

02 · Resident Progress

A clear path from lead to admitted.

Each resident profile shows a five-step journey: Captured, Evaluated, Prepared, Executed, and Activated. No guesswork about what's been completed and what's next.

03 · Document Preparation

Build and send the packet in minutes.

Enter resident basics, set the responsible party, and send the admission packet or resident agreement with one click. No printing, no scanning, no chasing down signatures.

04 · Document Signing

Every form, signed and stored.

Residents and families sign directly in a secure, branded viewer. Documents are automatically organized in the resident's vault the moment the signature is captured.
