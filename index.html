Any Questions?

Everything you'd ask on the first call.

How quickly can we go live?

Most communities are live with Highlands Virtual Assist within a week. Vital Connect timelines depend on your existing systems and we'll scope it on the first call.

Do your products integrate with our CRM?

Yes. Prospects and conversations are auto-organized and exportable to the CRMs senior living teams already use.

Is the chatbot really branded for our community?

Every widget is themed with your community's colors, logo, and tone. It feels like an extension of your team, not a third-party bot.

What does pricing look like?

We price per community with volume tiers for groups. Reach out and we'll send a tailored quote within 24 hours.

Are you HIPAA-aware?

Vital Connect is built with HIPAA-aware operations in mind. Virtual Assist captures only the lead information visitors choose to share.
